Vehicle Loss of Use Extension

Understanding Loss of Use: Reimburses hire car or replacement commercial rental costs incurred while a vehicle is undergoing accident repairs. Note: Most standard policies include a 7-day excess window before cover starts. Because repairers often provide basic courtesy cars, this option is less popular for general vehicles, but recommended for essential work vehicles/trucks your trade cannot operate without (provided replacement hire vehicles are available).
